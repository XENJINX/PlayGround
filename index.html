THE LOOP

Enemies follow the marked lane. Anything that reaches the exit damages core integrity. Upgrade the core, reforge its weapon, and choose a target priority. Survive all 20 waves.

REFORGING

You only ever have one tower. Replacing its form keeps your upgrade level and triggers a four-second overdrive (+35% fire rate). Form swap costs credits and has a short cooldown — change weapons to fit the wave.

BURN & CHILL

Blaze stacks a damage-over-time burn. Cryo slows targets and makes them take 30% more damage from every source. Bosses resist slow.
